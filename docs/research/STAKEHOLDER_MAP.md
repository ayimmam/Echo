# Stakeholder map and participation plan — Dimts

2026-10-03. **Planning baseline; participation and appointments are not yet confirmed.** Requested by the author alongside a research-publication output. This map covers development, STEM P1, Grade 2 P2, competition, publication and closure. It does not assert that any school, official, adviser, provider or university office has agreed to participate.

Anteneh F. Yimmam is the named project lead and intended lead research analyst. Person 2 and Person 3 are existing unnamed team roles, not confirmed additional recruits. The university's legal controller, formal principal investigator (PI), ethics-review route and data custodian must be appointed; a student lead is not automatically the institutional PI. Hawassa's [Research Affairs Directorate](https://www.hu.edu.et/research-affairs-directorate) describes university-wide research coordination; this supports asking it to identify the correct route, not assuming approval by a particular college's IRB.

## 1. What participation levels mean

These are proposed commitments, not measured availability. **Intensity and authority are separate:** a parent may spend little time on the project but still has an essential consent decision.

| Level | Expected involvement | Participation mode |
|---|---|---|
| Core | Weekly throughout development and writing; current budget assumes about 10 hours per student per week | Build, coordinate, analyse and document |
| High | Repeated involvement during an active field or governance phase | Co-design, participate, supervise or operate; no automatic access to all data |
| Medium | Scheduled consultations and specific milestone reviews | Advise, review, authorise within remit or provide a service |
| Low / conditional | Orientation, formal gate, results briefing or involvement only if a dependency is activated | Inform, consent, permit, evaluate or receive results |

Data tiers below are defined in the [research data management plan](RESEARCH_DATA_MANAGEMENT_PLAN.md): **R0** separated identity/consent/linkage records; **R1** restricted, pseudonymised study records in Ethiopia; **R2** disclosure-reviewed anonymous aggregate publication files; **S** synthetic or separately approved existing public assets. A role in this table is not an account entitlement.

## 3. Decision and accountability map

Each decision has one accountable role, to be named where still vacant. Consultation does not transfer consent rights. Separate prerequisite decisions remain separate.

| Decision | Accountable role | Responsible / consulted | Required boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Product scope and June schedule | Project lead — Anteneh | Team; supervisor and teacher partners consulted | Guardrails fixed; supervisor separately accepts thesis scope |
| Academic research question / analysis plan | Appointed academic PI | Anteneh + ML; pedagogy adviser | Freeze before held-out evaluation; negative results remain reportable |
| Institutional controller, access agreement and retention | Authorised university/controller representative | Custodian + legal/DPO; Anteneh and PI consulted | Named author access and post-graduation arrangements in writing before field data |
| Ethics approval for each cohort / amendment | Competent ethics committee | PI submits; schools/participants consulted | G4-U does not authorise P2 |
| Facility/site permission | Relevant authorised site authority | Lead + local coordinator | Institution/school/woreda permissions recorded separately where required |
| Individual participation / withdrawal | Each participant; guardian where applicable | Independent consent contact facilitates; child assent per approved protocol | No manager, researcher, parent-group vote or competition deadline substitutes for individual requirements |
| Start / pause / stop a session | Session teacher or instructor | Field researcher checks eligibility; any participant can raise a stop request | If any required permission/consent fails, no capture; no voice-based exclusion workaround |
| Grant R1 researcher access | Controller's authorised data-access approver | Custodian provisions; PI confirms named purpose/variables | Approved Ethiopia-based environment; grant Anteneh the agreed scope, not unrestricted teacher reports |
| Anonymous publication release | Controller's designated disclosure approver | Anteneh prepares; supervisor or qualified reviewer independently checks | Two people review; author cannot approve own export alone; no field-data cloud import |
| Public manuscript submission and author order | Designated corresponding author under institutional rules | All actual authors approve contribution statement and final draft | Privacy review checks disclosure, not whether findings are favourable; no promised authorship for access/funding |
| Operation or closure after defence | Named institutional custodian/service owner | Sys hands over; Lead verifies deletion/access schedule | Personal data has a finite approved retention date and a funded owner |

## 4. Participation schedule and burden

- **October 2026:** appoint supervisor/PI and controller/custodian; identify ethics route and school jurisdiction; two teacher co-design sessions; language/pedagogy reviews; agree Anteneh's research access and publication plan. Keep an internal contact/role register outside the public repository.
- **Before P1:** obtain G4-U, independent student consent arrangements, room permission and the approved research-data environment. Instructors/students receive an accessible explanation and a no-penalty decline route. Confirm rather than assume headcount and duration.
- **Before P2:** obtain G4-E and school/guardian/assent processes; train two observers, recruit development and held-out teachers separately, check classroom eligibility each session. Usefulness check-ins target ≤10 minutes weekly plus ≤20 minutes at exit; participation may be skipped. No new child assessment.
- **April–June 2027:** freeze data/analysis, return a plain-language pooled findings summary to participants when safe, complete thesis and manuscript draft, conduct disclosure review and agree handover. Officials receive no individual results. Publish only reviewed outputs.
- **After defence:** Anteneh's approved analysis/revision access continues for the agreed period; custodian reviews access monthly and at role changes. Publication is an aim, not a guaranteed acceptance. Proposed retention ends 30 June 2029, subject to approval before collection.

The existing 900-person-hour envelope is unchanged. Reserve **30 hours within the 120-hour ethics/field allocation** for stakeholder/access/consent administration and **30 within the 140-hour writing allocation** for data curation/disclosure/manuscript packaging. These are planning estimates. Observer attendance, reviewer time and travel must fit the remaining field capacity or be explicitly resourced; reduce optional sync/I-5 before adding participants or infrastructure. The [data plan](RESEARCH_DATA_MANAGEMENT_PLAN.md) defines a manually checked summary-transfer route, so research access does not require a new dashboard or export service.
