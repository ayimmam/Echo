## 2. Legal and governance basis

The [official Proclamation 1321/2024](https://justice.gov.et/en/law/personal-data-protection-proclamation/) addresses consent/withdrawal (Arts. 7–8), minors (Art. 11), local storage (Art. 22), and research safeguards/exceptions (Art. 54). Article 54 contains conditional research provisions; it is not a blanket exemption. The project chooses separate research consent and keeps all fixed guardrails. Obtain a written institutional interpretation, including Article 22's server/data-centre wording for phones and an offline workstation. Current implementing guidance still needs verification under WS-F.

[NIST SP 800-188](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.SP.800-188) distinguishes disclosure-risk assessment and controlled access from simply masking identifiers; it describes governance and alternatives to public data release. We use it as technical guidance, **not Ethiopian law**. The tier design, thresholds, dates and workflow below are project proposals, not requirements attributed to NIST or the Proclamation.

Before G4-U, the controller/PI/custodian and Anteneh sign an access and data-use record covering: named users and purpose; variables; physical/remote-access location; storage/backup/keys; consent/withdrawal; disclosure authority; access after graduation; actual retention dates; publication review turnaround and appeal; costs and closure. Extend scope separately at G4-E. If the agreement or environment is unavailable, use synthetic/approved existing assets only; never quietly substitute commercial cloud storage.

The locked model and each observer use the same protocol-defined observation periods. ## 5. Collection and author-access workflow

1. **Separate decisions:** capture/use of the app, optional derived sync and optional research contribution/publication are distinct consent scopes. No preselected research opt-in. Research refusal does not remove the teacher's local report or disadvantage students. Before a study session, confirm adult and classroom eligibility; research-only observation of non-consenting participants is not a workaround.
2. **Practical refusal:** the approved school/institutional contact confirms whole-room conditions without giving researchers child names. If someone declines and their voice cannot be excluded without identification, **do not capture that session**; use a separately consented session or a non-capture activity. Do not remove a child from ordinary teaching just to obtain data. Participants can request pause/stop without a public explanation.
3. **Small, teacher-approved contribution:** after a consented session, show/explain the study summary and permit “do not contribute this session” without deleting the private report. Default is no contribution until enrolled/authorised. For June, the minimum route is a coded paper/local form with numeric totals read from the teacher-authorised summary, checked by a second person. It requires no share sheet, email, bulk report export, web research portal or production database dump. No screenshots of reports.
4. **Observer independence:** lock each observer's coded form before model values are copied. Session codes assigned by the custodian pair forms; only the required aggregate fields enter R1. Keep incorrect/missing reference flags instead of manufacturing agreement. The saved `LessonRecord` is not retroactively mutated by contribution or withdrawal preferences; research inclusion is a separate restricted ledger.
5. **Verify and admit:** two-person check transcription of numeric summaries; validate units, denominators, versions, cohort, consent scope, duplicate session codes and held-out allocation. Custodian removes direct identifiers and exact dates before admission to R1. Transfer physical forms securely or use an approved encrypted local device; never ordinary messaging. An electronic export is optional later and must pass the same allowlist/security tests.
6. **Give Anteneh usable access:** provision his named account/approved device, dictionary, source snapshot and working analysis directory; verify he can open the supplied tables and reproduce a synthetic example before G4. During collection, provide a checked snapshot at least weekly. Access does not depend on internet sync or on an official dashboard. Keep version, checksum, authorisation, access and release logs without report contents.
7. **Freeze and analyse:** by 15 April 2027, version the consent-filtered dataset, exclusions, model/observer gates and analysis protocol; write reproducible analysis later as implementation work. A later withdrawal creates a superseding snapshot and invalidates affected working copies/results; “frozen” does not defeat withdrawal. Never recalibrate on held-out results.
8. **Prepare R2:** Anteneh derives candidate aggregates inside Ethiopia; a second reviewer performs §6 and the authorised controller representative approves release. Target ten working days for review and record reasons for rejection; escalate delays to the institutional research office. This is a proposed service agreement, not an assumed university policy. The approved package belongs in his publication workspace; no further ad hoc permissions for each rerun inside the agreed scope.

Minimum package delivered to Anteneh: restricted numeric tables (CSV or equivalent) + dictionary/codebook + separate frozen split metadata + analysis/provenance manifest + missingness/exclusion summary; and a distinct **R2 publication package** containing only approved aggregate CSV tables, figures, methods/dictionary and release approval. Actual filenames/location are assigned in the access agreement, outside this public code repository. A file called “anonymous.csv” is not evidence of anonymisation.

## 6. Anonymisation and publication release rule

Publication is a **fixed release**, not a dashboard with arbitrary filters. Apply this proposed checklist to the thesis, appendices, manuscript, slides and supplementary files together:

- Remove participant/session/observer codes, exact dates, named schools/rooms, rare job/demographic details and linkable filenames/metadata. Do not publish individual trajectories, teacher-level scatterplots, quotations, example private reports or lesson rows. Model-versus-reference error is a measurement result, not a teacher performance score.
- For teacher-derived result cells, require at least **five distinct consenting teachers/instructors** as a minimum screen, with teacher-balanced contributions. Count people, not lessons or ten-minute periods. Examine dominance, recognisable extremes, small response categories, denominators, complements and what tables reveal jointly. Five alone is not an anonymity guarantee.
- Assess likely linkage using public school/competition information and local knowledge. Review overlapping cohorts, earlier thesis/slides/preprints and revised releases for subtraction/differencing. Record the release, audience, transformations, residual risk and reviewers. Generalise, suppress or withhold outputs that fail; do not merely delete names.
- Never pool STEM and Grade 2 to clear a disclosure threshold. Keep per-language validity analysis in R1 when groups are too small to release; do not replace it with a misleading pooled validity claim. Public reports state that subgroup evidence is restricted or unavailable and limit claims accordingly. A non-sensitive overall recruitment count can be considered separately, but must pass linkage review; never publish false counts.
- Preserve scientific meaning: suppression/generalisation must be labelled. Do not change observations to make results look stronger or pretend synthetic results are real. A disclosure reviewer must approve the entire package; Anteneh alone cannot certify it.

**Small-sample conflict:** the present ≥4 held-out-teacher minimum may produce **no releasable teacher-derived validation table**; ≥6 usability teachers still may not support language breakdowns or rare response counts. Five is not a demand to expand the study automatically. First use pooled safe usability summaries and non-personal engineering results; examine restricted evidence under approved in-country access where the university permits. If meaningful publication of model agreement requires a larger sample, secure resources and ethics amendment before recruitment/data freeze, or narrow the paper. Do not trade consent or anonymity for a publication claim.

R2 is a specifically authorised publication exception to ambiguous historical wording “no pilot-derived export”: it permits only reviewed anonymous aggregate **scholarly outputs**, not research microdata, weights, features, logs or a route into Huawei Cloud. Competition cloud remains synthetic-only; a manuscript's existence does not make its underlying R1 data a public asset. All personal data and its processing remain in Ethiopia. This is within the user guardrails, not a cross-border personal-data permission.

**Proposed data-availability statement, to use only after its facts become true:** “The approved aggregate results and analysis materials accompany this work. Participant-level derived measurements are restricted because of consent and re-identification risks in a small school sample. Approved verification may be arranged through the institutional custodian within Ethiopia; access is subject to the study approvals and is not guaranteed. No classroom audio, speech transcripts or child-level records were retained. Synthetic fixtures support public reproduction of the software, not independent verification of private field observations.” Replace “accompany” with the actual available location/status; never publish a promise of unrestricted data on request.

Retention extension/new research purpose needs a reviewed amendment and consent/legal decision before expiry.
