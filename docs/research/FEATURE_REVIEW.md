# Feature review — Echovolve / Dimts

Review dates: **2026-10-02–03**. Decision horizon: **June 2027 defence**; competition milestones may be earlier. **Design recommendations, not implementation or measured results.** [ADR-016](../adr/016-defence-scope-and-no-recording.md) records the scope decision. Existing uncommitted infrastructure work is preserved.

## Executive summary

Build a private, offline reflection aid around **capture quality and a conditionally validated adult/child talk measure**. Do not make eight indicators, two accurate models, a live district service and a new classroom-audio corpus prerequisites for graduation. The evidence supports specific practices and sustained support, not the proposition that more dashboard numbers improve literacy ([Coach][coach]; [PRIMR ICT trial][primr]).

**Top five adds**

1. **A1: A teacher-selected weekly practice card**, with one concrete action and optional Tried / Not yet / Not useful follow-up, entirely offline.
2. **A2: A 30-second capture-readiness check**, showing clipping, interruptions and placement guidance without saving sound or claiming to certify child audibility.
3. **A3: One-tap lesson context and exclusion**, so demonstration, practice, another adult or playback do not silently produce misleading advice.
4. **A4: Pause/resume and discard-before-save**, with visible microphone state and honest deletion status on a shared phone.
5. **A5: A no-recording field-validation protocol**, with independent observers, a validity stop rule and consented author access/anonymous publication outputs under ADR-017.

**Top five removes / defers**

1. **Remove persisted-audio Research Mode**, its upload/annotation service and the promised new classroom recording corpus: they violate this review's fixed RAM-only guardrail.
2. **Defer real woreda/school aggregate releases and director-sharing accounts**; demonstrate suppression using synthetic records only.
3. **Defer production LID / I-6**, including language-policy monitoring; retain only a bounded, separately labelled public-corpus experiment.
4. **Defer child-single/choral models and I-2/I-3/I-4/I-7/I-8 in teacher reports**; retain their documented definitions and existing fixtures as future contracts.
5. **Remove the mandatory multi-framework/distillation/cloud-training stack**; use one small baseline and one measured runtime. Defer the unrelated roadmap; remove forced-alignment child diagnostics.

The first three are substantial cuts. Teacher trust and a finishable thesis win over breadth; the competition still receives a real AI path, a Huawei Cloud visual demonstration and reproducible evidence. Eligibility of a synthetic-only cloud demonstration remains an author/organiser question, not a fact established by this review.

## 2. Evidence that changes the scope

### 2.1 Feedback: what changed, and what did not

- **Automated talk-time feedback:** the supplied M-Powering Teachers RCT studied **414 mentors in US-dominated online 1:1 instruction**. Table 2 estimates a talk-ratio coefficient of **−0.035** against control mean **0.722**: **3.5 percentage points, about 4.8% relative**, not five percentage points. The intervention bundled several feedback elements, so this is not an isolated causal effect of a talk-time gauge. Satisfaction/optimism outcomes are not an Ethiopian literacy test. Engagement fell from 74% after the first session to around 30% later; very few used reflection text boxes. Correct the earlier audit/README rather than duplicate the source. [Demszky & Liu (2023)][mpower]
- **TeachFX in physical classrooms:** the published 2025 report describes 224 Utah mathematics/science teachers, a 20% increase in targeted focusing questions and no effect on other teaching practices. Accuracy skepticism, privacy concerns and time constrained use. The earlier working paper describes 523 recruited users; do not mix its sample denominator with the published analysis. This supports a narrow actionable focus and measuring engagement; it does not validate acoustic detection of questions. [Published article][teachfxtrial]; [author working paper][teachfxwp]
- **Human observation is broader:** Teach Primary includes classroom culture, instruction and socioemotional practices as well as time use. A microphone cannot establish understanding, inclusion or instructional correctness. Coach’s guidance (developed with consultation including the Building Evidence in Education network) connects observation to reflection, modelling, practice and a jointly chosen goal, with focused feedback. Adopt the workflow principle, not Teach scores or an automated Teach replacement. [Teach Primary][teach]; [Coach guidance][coach]
- **LMIC learning evidence concerns packages:** 3ie's review includes structured pedagogy combining materials, training and support. Kenya's PRIMR ICT trial found no significant additional literacy gain from the tested ICT investments over the base instructional programme. Therefore the app should help teachers use existing approved practices; it should not expand into a curriculum generator or claim to replace coaching. [3ie review][threeie]; [RTI trial][primr]
- **EdTech Hub’s review of 170 studies in 40 LMICs** finds promising support for reflection and peer learning but limited evidence about sustained classroom change, literacy outcomes and cost. Its recommendation to co-design and retain human relationships supports optional use with existing CPD colleagues, not a new mentor portal. [EdTech Hub policy brief][edtech]
- **Digital support is not automatically equivalent to visits:** J-PAL's synthesis reports stronger literacy outcomes from in-person than virtual coaching in South Africa, and examples where reminders did not change practice. This is evidence against adding reminders, badges or portals as a substitute for usable feedback. It is not proof that this offline app will fail. [J-PAL synthesis][jpal]

### 2.2 Ethiopia: fit the system teachers already inhabit

The supplied EGRA report supports low and uneven reading performance and **associations**, not causal effects, between observation and reading. Its 2016 Sidaamu Afoo caveat matters. ESAA 2024/25 excludes Amhara from national indicators and reports Grades 1–6 section sizes averaging 54.2 nationally and 59.6 in Sidama. Neither document measures ownership of suitable teacher phones. Retain these caveats; avoid "nobody observes teachers" and universal novelty claims. [EGRA supplied report](papers/19184396_early-grade-reading-assessment-egra-2018-endline-report.pdf); [ESAA supplied report](<papers/MoE Annual Abstract 2024-2025.pdf>)

School-based self/joint study, cluster interaction and external support appear in MoE's historical CPD structure. Use a teacher-owned on-screen practice card for an **optional conversation with an existing mentor**, with no official account or automatic report transfer. ESDP VI covers 2020/21–2024/25, so it is historical planning context by this review date; current implementation and local cluster availability require interviews. [MoE ESDP V, p.59][esdpv]; [ESDP VI primary document record][esdpvi]

The historical 1994 Education and Training Policy §3.5 supports nationality-language primary education. A Refworld record labelled **2023** actually links to that **1994** PDF; it cannot verify current policy. Confirm the current national text and regional Grade 2 practice; no language-compliance feature is justified by the historical document. [Policy PDF, title page and §3.5][policy1994]

READ M&E is the source of assessment findings; READ II is an intervention programme, not interchangeable evidence. EDC lists READ II’s duration as 2018–2024 and describes teacher support and literacy work, but a programme page is not an impact evaluation. The app can reference locally approved guides after permission and native-speaker review; do not assume READ II funds or staff remain available. [EDC READ II][readii]

Internet penetration and smartphone ownership are different measures. World Bank's 2025 project results report 33% unique mobile penetration and 19% internet access in 2024; GSMA’s indexed Figure 15 reports 45% urban versus 13% rural smartphone ownership among surveyed adults in 2024 (full PDF fetch exceeded the tool’s size limit; verify the chart before final thesis citation). These do **not** establish ownership, Android version, RAM or charging access among Hawassa teachers. Survey actual participants and budget loan phones before assuming bring-your-own-device. [World Bank digital results][connect]; [GSMA survey][gsma]

### 2.3 Device and ML feasibility

- **VAD is the most plausible cheap primitive.** Silero's maintainers report an approximately 2 MB JIT model and under 1 ms per 30+ ms chunk on one CPU thread. This is a maintainer benchmark, not a 2 GB A53/A55 Android result or battery measurement. Use a VAD/energy baseline in P0/P1; do not confuse speech activity with adult/child/teacher classification. [Silero source and benchmark][silero]
- **Voice type is not role or chorus.** VTC predicts key child/other child/adult voice types in child-centred recordings, not classroom teacher identity or single-versus-choral response. Its multilingual training is useful precedent, not a ready five-class classroom model. A 2025 follow-up found only marginal gains from architecture/features and stronger gains from relevant data: dropping data collection does not make this ML task easy. [Kunze et al.][vtc2025] Donnelly's supplied five instructional-segment system also uses speech/language features and a different label taxonomy. [VTC][vtc]; [Donnelly][donnelly]
- **LID has a size and domain gap.** MMS-LID-256 has one billion parameters: roughly **4 GB at FP32 or 1 GB at INT8 for weights alone** (parameter-count calculation, excluding activations/runtime). It is not the promised ≤8 MB on-device student. `amh`, `sid`, `eng` availability and public adult corpora do not establish 3-second noisy classroom accuracy, code-switch handling or calibrated `other` detection. No verified target-phone latency or battery figure was found for an Amharic/Sidaamu Afoo classroom LID student. [MMS model card][mms]
- **Budgets remain targets:** combined models ≤20 MB; end-to-end RTF ≤0.3; RSS ≤300 MB; ≤10 battery percentage points/40 minutes. Measure the whole release pipeline, screen state, phone/OS/page size, thermal conditions and longest STEM sessions. Memory-mapped model size is not app RSS; a desktop RTF is not an Android benchmark. [Existing device test plan](../testing/POST_IMPLEMENTATION_TEST_PLAN.md)
- Keep MindSpore Lite as the first runtime candidate, one convolution-only student and owned streaming features. Run a bounded export/device spike; if it fails, compare the same graph on one fallback runtime and ship only one. Distillation, recurrent challengers and ModelArts are deferred post-defence; any later experiment needs a measured benefit and lawful data. [Lite operators][lite]; [ONNX mobile][onnx]

### 2.5 Law, trust and comparable systems

The Ministry of Justice now provides an official primary copy of Proclamation 1321/2024, resolving the audit's source-location gap for the official host. Article 22 requires local storage of locally obtained personal data; the project's no-export boundary is deliberately stronger than merely satisfying storage locality. Transient processing is not automatically exempt, teacher pseudonyms do not anonymise records, and consent/child-data/retention duties remain relevant without an audio file. Obtain university decisions on controller, lawful basis, children, registration, DPIA/DPO and breach response before field use. [Official law and Gazette][law]

Follow-up found ECA’s indexed **PDP Registration User Guide** and **PDP Manual Draft v1.1.3**, covering registration, DPIA, breach notice and data-subject requests. Direct fetches timed out; the draft label is explicit. This is evidence of published operational guidance, not verification of a binding implementing directive or its current final version. [ECA guide][ecaguide]; [draft manual][ecamanual] **Do not infer none exist**, claim an exemption, or rely on a vendor's statement that enforcement has not begun. Update WS-F with the current manual/directive and exact version before launch. Article 22(1) specifically refers to a server or data centre: **whether a wholly local phone deployment suffices needs a written legal answer**. Offline engineering may proceed with approved fixtures; personal-data field use waits for that answer or a compliant in-country derived-data arrangement. [Official Gazette][law]; [ECA PDP portal][eca]

TeachFX and LENA are useful contrasts, not guardrail-compatible implementations: their own privacy pages describe stored classroom/daylong recordings and subsequent processing. LENA's deletion after processing is different from never writing audio. [TeachFX policy][teachfxprivacy]; [LENA policy][lena]

No primary-source account was verified of TeachFX, TalkMoves, DART or LENA being abandoned specifically because of surveillance or maintenance. Do not fabricate a shutdown story from a stale site or the end of a research grant. There **is** direct evidence of non-use, time burden, distrust of accuracy and privacy concerns in the TeachFX study, and hardware/setup limitations in Jensen. The Israeli CCTV study informs surveillance perception but is not Ethiopian evidence or an audio-product failure report. [TeachFX trial][teachfxtrial]; [Jensen][jensen]; [Birnhack & Perry-Hazan][trust]

## 4. Concrete additions and removals

### A1 — One practice, one action, one response

Add a bundled, reviewed set of **three** practice cards: (a) invite an explanation after an answer; (b) alternate a teacher demonstration with a short pupil practice opportunity; (c) choose deliberately between a group response and a volunteer response for a particular activity. These are candidate scripts to co-design, not proven Ethiopia-specific prescriptions. Each has a 40–70-word local-language example, and an optional prompt the teacher can discuss with an existing CPD colleague **on their own screen**. No mentor login, report export, student example or speech-derived text. The teacher can choose a different card or none. Coach supports the focused cycle, not these exact scripts. [Coach][coach]

**Selection rule v1:** show the teacher's chosen card, retain it until they change or dismiss it, and ask once at the next weekly opening: Tried / Not yet / Not useful / Skip. No selection means offer the three titles, without selecting a "weakness" for the teacher. If Not useful, offer a different title and stop repeating the dismissed card. No streaks, achievement badges, mandatory journaling, notification campaign or server engagement tracking.

For an **observations panel beside the chosen card**, require a released indicator and at least **three eligible lessons on two days**, each with **≥10 observed minutes and ≥80% capture coverage**, in the same chosen grade/subject and compatible model/spec. Display at most I-1 plus I-5, their coverage and number of eligible lessons. Their values never select or change the card; there is no claim the chosen practice follows from a high/low ratio. These numerical eligibility values are **provisional design safeguards**, to freeze on development/usability evidence before field testing; they are not literature cut-offs. If insufficient, say "Not enough reliable observations this week" and leave the self-selected practice available without saying the audio identified a need. No healthy talk-ratio band, comparative colour, teacher rank, or inferred improvement. A quiet lesson cannot trigger admonishment. This replaces the old range-to-suggestion mapping, while keeping the existing one-focus rule. [TeachFX trial][teachfxtrial]

### A2 — Capture readiness, not an accuracy certificate

Before a lesson, offer a **30-second RAM-only** check. Report permission/mic status, clipping fraction and observed/missing duration; the ≤10-second ring continuously overwrites. Include a short placement illustration and an explicit reminder that a nearby adult sounding clear does not establish audibility at the back of the room. A failed permission, no samples or known OS silencing blocks capture; clipping is advisory until a device-tested threshold exists. No calibrated decibel/exposure claims. Compare desk and wired mic **for both adult and child signal usefulness** during authorised field observation before recommending equipment. A lavalier may improve teacher audio while losing pupils, biasing talk share. [Android input sharing][android]; [Jensen][jensen]; [Schlotterbeck][schlotterbeck]

### A3 — Context and disagreement cost one tap

At End lesson, offer optional chips: **mainly demonstration / mixed / mainly pupil practice / unsure**, plus **only the teacher’s adult voice and no played speech / another adult or playback / unsure** (default unsure) and **exclude this lesson from reflection**. Store only enumerated values locally; no free-text lesson account. Another adult/playback or unsure makes teacher-proxy I-1/I-5 unavailable; it is not a classifier correction. Exclusion preserves the original measurement for the teacher until they delete it, but removes it from their weekly eligibility set. It does not create training labels. Defer editing already-synced immutable records: initial context is sealed before save; later exclusions are local report preferences, with no server report presented as authoritative. [Teach Primary][teach]; [teacher privacy study][trust]

### A4 — Pause and discard are operational privacy

Pause stops microphone acquisition, clears audio/features/model state and records only a MISSING duration. Resume requires the visible app and starts fresh streaming state. End offers Save summary or Discard; Discard clears the unsaved result and creates no outbox record. The saved report remains protected by the planned account lock. Withdrawal immediately stops local capture and pending uploads; remote purge is **pending until acknowledged**, including offline scenarios. Never promise recall of a screenshot or already shared copy. These functions make the existing consent principle usable without a new backend service. [Android][android]; [law][law]

### A5 — Replace the unavailable research corpus with bounded live validation

**Scope assumption:** the RAM-only rule governs all new project capture, including research and staged sessions. Already existing lawfully held public acoustic corpora may be studied in an approved environment; this is not permission to record new voices or persist captured features. If the author intends the rule to prohibit even pre-existing corpus access, the coarse-model research must narrow further to approved pretrained inference and feasibility work.

No new classroom recording, encrypted audio file, research upload, replay buffer longer than ten seconds, saved features, soft-label dump or persisted embeddings. Already published, lawfully usable corpora may support offline acoustic development; do not transcribe, import speech text as a feature, or claim they represent Ethiopian classrooms. This applies the guardrail to **all newly captured project audio**, not only the release APK; a separate recorder is not a workaround.

Prepare an observer guide and paper/in-country forms; use two independent observers for **at least 25%** of field sessions. For I-1, observers separately time broad exclusive adult/child/overlap/non-speech categories with a stopwatch and retain lesson totals plus uncertainty/unsupported-condition flags. For I-5, independently tally alternations in fixed ten-minute periods and retain counts, not speech content or child identities. Observers must not see model results while coding. Use protocol-defined common observation periods and retain only common-window durations/counts and missingness flags; no fine timeline. Unalignable model/observer comparisons are unusable. Development sessions establish repeatability; freeze the model/rules before a separate teacher/school test allocation. These are a proposed protocol and staffing target, not a validated replacement for frame annotation. [Donnelly live observation precedent][donnelly]; [Teach observer training][teach]

Release only a measure whose human reference is itself usable. Initial project criteria: observer-to-observer I-1 difference ≤5 pp on at least 80% of paired sessions, then model-vs-observer I-1 MAE ≤5 pp with uncertainty reported per language. Use at least eight held-out lessons from four teachers (at least two teachers per language), disjoint from development teachers and, where recruitment permits, schools; paired observation covers at least 25% and at least four held-out lessons. This is a feasibility floor, not enough for a precise population estimate. For I-5, observer differences must be ≤max(2 alternations, 10% of the paired mean) in at least 80% of paired ten-minute periods; model count MAE must be ≤max(2, 20% of mean reference count) separately in each language. Report counts, distributions and teacher-cluster uncertainty, not a pass alone. Missing strata, unreliable observers or insufficient data leave the relevant measure unavailable. These proposed thresholds must be frozen before the held-out phase; they establish a pilot release decision, not general validation. If observers cannot reliably measure a construct, **remove its numerical claim from the field report**, rather than lower the bar on the test set. At least six teachers over three weeks is a **feasibility/usability sample**, not a power calculation or proof of learning impact. Frame-F1, choral F1 and subsecond latency claims require suitable existing labelled corpora and must be named as corpus results, never field validation. Agree the reduced thesis question with the supervisor.

**Author-requested research output (2026-10-03):** the [stakeholder map](STAKEHOLDER_MAP.md) and [data plan](RESEARCH_DATA_MANAGEMENT_PLAN.md), draft ADR-017, make Anteneh's named study-data access and a thesis/manuscript draft explicit. R1 paired/coded measurements remain restricted in Ethiopia; R2 anonymous publication tables need independent review. App/sync consent does not grant research access; teachers may contribute minimal summaries separately, with no automatic card/report extraction. Use checked numeric forms and participant-selected questionnaires, never speech-derived interview notes. The four-teacher validity floor is not an anonymous-publication guarantee: per-language results may remain restricted. No new portal or recorded corpus is added.

### Removed commitments and why

- **F39/F40/F41: recorded Research Mode, new audio corpus and media annotation service.** This is a direct guardrail conflict, independent of whether consent or encryption might make recording lawful. It also removes upload retry/storage/retention/annotation operations. The cost is reduced model adaptation and weaker temporal ground truth; A5 must report that limit. Keep the old plans labelled superseded rather than silently erasing the decision. [Original data design](../DIMTS_ARCHITECTURE_CONTEXT.md#92-data-sources); [law][law]
- **F30: fine-tuning on newly recorded classroom speech.** Without that corpus this promise is not credible. Existing approved acoustic corpora and live aggregate evaluation remain; no on-device learning or stored posterior/timeline substitute is introduced. If no suitable adult/child model exists by the January gate, ship only descriptive speech activity and the teacher-chosen cards as a feasibility prototype. This may reduce thesis/competition strength, which must be disclosed. [VTC][vtc]
- **F31: mandatory distillation and multiple training stacks.** Neither teacher impact nor the captured rubric depends on using a specific compression technique. Start with one model; restore a distillation experiment only if an approved corpus, exact licence and measured failure of the simple baseline justify it within the time budget. No claim that distillation automatically escapes the teacher model's licence. [MMS][mms]; [competition capture](<papers/Huawei Talent Competition details.pdf>)
- **F69/F70: required openGauss/openEuler and a separate SPA.** Preserve ADR-015: one capability-selected backend, one tested engine and rendered templates. OS branding and extra frontends provide no established additional rubric points. A supported openEuler VPS remains an option, not a requirement. [ADR-015](../adr/015-capability-gated-hosting.md); [competition capture](<papers/Huawei Talent Competition details.pdf>)
- **F80: forced-alignment child micro-diagnostics.** This changes the product into individual assessment and entails matching speech to linguistic content, conflicting with the no speech-derived text/child-identification scope. Remove it from this product roadmap rather than presenting it as an inevitable next feature. General authored practice text is still allowed. [Fixed constraints](../DIMTS_ARCHITECTURE_CONTEXT.md#6-hard-constraints)

**Deferrals with the largest trade-offs:** LID has high competition novelty but no verified local coaching effect or cheap-phone model; feasibility wins. Choral/single detail needs data the guardrail rules out collecting; honest coarse output wins. District reporting could eventually support CPD planning, but six teachers cannot sustain rich safe grade/language cells and support a teacher-owned pilot at once; trust wins. O-class, all subjects, generated materials, hearing screening and HarmonyOS each add a new validation or maintenance domain; the Grade 2 reading build wins. [MMS][mms]; [security](../security/ARCHITECTURE.md); [Teach][teach]

## 5. Guardrail costs

- **No ASR:** loses automated checks of question quality, uptake, explanation and content; these are central to the positive TeachFX findings. Keep useful acoustic descriptions and authored practice cards; do not relabel acoustic timing as semantic pedagogy. [TeachFX trial][teachfxtrial]
- **No retained audio:** loses replay, granular annotation, test-set reanalysis and classroom-specific fine-tuning. Live observer totals provide narrower evidence. No Research Mode exception is recommended. [Donnelly][donnelly]; [original evaluation plan](RESEARCH_PLAN.md)
- **No child identity:** response counts cannot measure equitable participation, unique children or who was left out. Neither loudness nor single-child turns repairs that limitation. [VTC][vtc]
- **Teacher ownership/no comparison:** management cannot use individual reports to target or assess a teacher. A teacher may discuss a card voluntarily; no score or comparative dashboard is introduced. [trust][trust]
- **Ethiopia-only personal data:** adds local hosting/access/backup work and prevents reproducing private field evidence on Huawei Cloud. Reproduce the public demo using synthetic records and separately approved acoustic assets; do not export field weights or logs. [law][law]; [competition capture](<papers/Huawei Talent Competition details.pdf>)
